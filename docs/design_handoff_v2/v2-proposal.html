v2 — four changes

A modernisation pass, not a redesign. Colour, type, voice and every section’s copy are unchanged. Three of the four changes are token-level, so they land everywhere at once. Changes 1 and 2 are site-only; 3 and 4 affect the app too.

The token changes live in tokens/v2-modern.css, which is not imported by styles.css — the faithful recreation of the current product stays intact. Link the overlay after styles.css to preview.

Change 1 · structural

One dark band

The site is warm off-white end to end, which reads honest but flat. A single near-black section gives the page a spine and a place for the brand statement to land properly. One per page — a second would make it a dark theme, which it isn’t.

It replaces the light closing section that held “You can’t outrun your easy days.” Moss lifts to #8FB08F on dark; the faithful #6B8E6B goes muddy below 20% luminance. The band is text-only — the statement is the moment, and anything beside it competes.

Now — closing section
background: #F3F0EB
ink: #1A1A1A
accent: #6B8E6B
Proposed — dark band
--ground: #1A1A1A
--on-ground: #F3F0EB
--moss-on-ground: #8FB08F
Change 2 · structural

Real app screens, in a device frame

All three reference sites lead with phone screenshots. The current hero uses CSS approximations of the session card, which are on-palette but obviously not the product. The v2 hero renders the actual Today screen inside a device shell.

It is the real screen from the app UI kit, scaled — not a screenshot and not a rebuilt mockup, so it cannot drift from the product as it changes. The frame mirrors the app shell: status bar, content area, and the bottom nav pinned.

The crop is measured, not fixed: the content area sizes itself to the bottom of a named anchor element (Today’s “Log this session” button) plus a 32px gap, and the fade occupies exactly that gap. So the shot always ends on the session and its action, and the fade can never wash across content, whatever the screen’s content does later.

Open question: a second frame showing Coach inside the dark band rendered with a dark screen ground and I have not solved it yet, so the dark band is text-only. Frames work correctly on light sections.

Change 3 · token

Radii up one step

12px on a card is a 2019 default; 16px is where premium products sit now. This is the cheapest of the three and the one that touches the most surfaces — every card in the app and on the site, with no markup change.

Now
sm 8 · md 12 · lg 16 · xl 20
card-primary 12 · contextual 14 · data 10
Proposed
sm 10 · md 14 · lg 18 · xl 22
card-primary 16 · contextual 14 · data 12
Change 4 · token · app-facing

A two-layer card shadow

The current card shadow is a single 0 1px 2px at 4% — effectively invisible, so cards sit flat on the warm ground and the app reads as a document rather than an interface. The replacement keeps that 1px contact shadow to seat the card, and adds a wide, heavily-offset soft layer to do the lifting.

Both layers stay well under the threshold where a shadow reads as decoration. You should feel it and not see it. This is the largest perceived-quality change of the four, and the one that touches the most surfaces — every card in the app.

Now
0 1px 2px rgba(26,26,26,.04)
Proposed — --shadow-card
0 1px 2px rgba(26,26,26,.04),
0 10px 28px -10px rgba(26,26,26,.10)

Implementation note: the components set background: var(--card) inline with no shadow, so the token alone does nothing — each card component needs box-shadow: var(--shadow-card) added where it already sets that background. The app preview fakes this with an inline-style selector so it can be judged; that selector is a preview device, not the implementation.

Recommended, not built

More air

Section gaps 28px → 36px. Premium reads as space more than anything else, and this is the cheapest remaining lever. It is in the overlay as --section-gap: 36px but cannot be previewed by a token change: the screens set their gaps inline, so seeing it means editing the six screen files.

Also outstanding: an FAQ accordion and a facts band on the site, hero type up to ~68px, the Today hero line 44px → 52px, and replacing the bottom nav’s placeholder dots with the app’s real IconToday / IconPlan / IconCoach / IconStrava set.
