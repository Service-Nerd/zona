# Miles — open-lens review (Design Board, 2026-09-22)

**Screenshots:** all 17 in `docs/competitor/miles/`, viewed with **no item list**.
**Supersedes nothing.** `MILES-GAP-ANALYSIS.md` stands; this is the pass it could not be.

> ## Why this document exists
>
> 🔴 **The Phase-1 analysis was a checklist, by construction.** `T-01…T-20` came from
> `miles-teardown-brief.md`'s own screenshot index — **one item per screenshot, decided
> before anyone looked.** Every verdict answers *"how do we score against this item?"*
> Not one answers *"what is on this screen that nobody thought to ask about?"*
>
> The founder challenged this directly: *"Has a review of all miles screenshots been done
> with an open lens? It seems like there's just been a focus on change plan."* **He was
> right.** This pass found nine things the checklist had no slot for, and two of them are
> the most important observations in the whole teardown.

## 1. The two findings that change the competitive picture

### 1.1 🔴 THEY ARE SAYING OUR THING, ON THEIR PLAN REVEAL

`IMG_7181` — the card-stack. The bar chart of the eight-week block carries a **handwritten
annotation pointing at the two short weeks**:

> *easier on purpose*

And `IMG_7180`, their plan-building loading screen, narrates four steps. The last one, the
one still spinning, is:

> *Making recovery part of the plan*

And `IMG_7182`, their plan preview, prescribes the first easy run as:

> **Easy Run** · *Not faster than 13:12/mi*

**That is Zonna's entire argument, shipped by the competitor, in three places, before the
paywall.** "You're trying hard, that's the problem" · the deload is deliberate · the easy
day has a **ceiling**, not a target.

⚠️ **T-09 rated the loading screen PARTIAL — "ours is better written, theirs is better
drawn."** That is a judgement about craft. It missed that the *content* is our positioning.
A checklist scores the slot; it cannot notice the competitor has taken your sentence.

**What this does NOT mean.** It is not a reason to change our positioning, and it is not a
reason to panic: they say it in three places and then sell "Live coaching on every run" and
"Show up ready on race day", which is a performance promise. **The idea is unprotected, the
product built on it is not.** But *"we're the only ones who say this"* is no longer true and
must not be said internally or externally.

### 1.2 🔴 THEIR EXIT OFFER ABANDONS THEIR OWN TYPEFACE

`IMG_7184`. The whole app is a geometric sans. The exit offer is set in a **serif display
face** — "SAVE 64%", "Before you go, Russell." Nothing else in 17 screenshots uses it.

Plus: a struck-through **£155.88/year** anchor, which is the monthly rate annualised and a
price they have never charged; and *"It expires when you leave this screen."*

**The gap analysis caught the £24 undercut and stopped there.** The stronger finding is
that **the paywall was evidently built by a different hand with a different visual system**,
and it is visible to anyone who scrolls two screens. A user who notices has learned the
brand is a costume.

## 2. What they do WELL — and what we take

| | What it is | Take? |
|---|---|---|
| **The row pattern, everywhere** | `icon in tinted square · title · consequence subtitle · current value · chevron`. Identical on settings (`7186`), modify sheet (`7188`), profile (`7185`) | ✅ **Take the STRUCTURE.** See R-2/R-3 |
| **Consequence subtitles** | *"Shift the whole plan forward or back"* · *"Anchor your weekly endurance run"* · *"Balance of easy vs hard sessions"* | ✅ **The single most transferable thing in the set.** It teaches the runner what the lever does |
| **Icons that encode DATA** | `7175` ability tiers: a signal-bar glyph that **fills progressively** — 1 bar Beginner → 4 bars Elite. The icon carries the ordinal | ✅ **This is the icon argument that wins.** Not decoration; a second channel |
| **Semantic icon tints** | `7186`: tan for voice/coach, green for system, **red-tinted for Training Zones (heart rate)** | ⚠️ Take cautiously — we have six session colours already |
| **The dashed-border escape card** | `7175`: *"I'm just getting started — Not running 5k yet. I'll switch you to a run-walk start."* Dashed border = a different KIND of option, subordinate but not hidden, and it **branches** (chevron) rather than selects | ✅ **Take.** We have no visual grammar for "this option changes the flow" |
| **Skip is a grey text link ABOVE a disabled primary** | `7177`, `7179`. Every time | ✅ **They got right what our S4 found we got wrong.** Corroborates the ruling |
| **The trial timeline** | `7183`: *Today — full access · Day 5 — reminder that your trial ends soon*, as an icon-and-connector list | ✅ **Take.** It removes "when am I charged?" anxiety. We have a 14-day reverse trial and no such artefact |
| **Live count feedback** | `7178`: *"**3** days available"* in accent, updating as you tap | ✅ Cheap, and it closes the loop on a multi-select |
| **Progressive disclosure on a toggle** | `7178`: "I also cross-train" reveals a chip set only when on | ✅ Already our idiom; worth naming |
| **Plan length at the choice point** | `7174`: *"10K · 10–16 week plan"* under the option | ✅ Sets the expectation before the commitment |
| **Referral code IN the onboarding flow** | `7179` | 🔴 **See §5 — this may be our charity funnel's whole problem** |
| **The ticket-stub perforation** | `7182`: dashed rule with two notch cut-outs joining the dark stat block to the white explainer | ⚠️ Charming, and it is chrome. Collins wants it; Silvanto does not |

## 3. What they do BADLY — where we capitalise

| | Evidence | Our position |
|---|---|---|
| **A projection citing a source it does not have** | `7187` *"PROJECTED FINISH 1:04:37 – 1:10:01 · Projected from your logged runs"* while `7185` shows **0 runs** | 🟢 §109 forbids prediction. **We hold. And our rule needs a second half: the derivation must EXIST, not merely be named** |
| **Developer language in runner copy** | `7176`: *"It just replaces the old **hardcoded defaults** until we have better data from a run."* | 🟢 A runner should never read "hardcoded defaults". Our benchmark copy is already better (T-05) |
| **Illegible hero** | `7172`: "PLAN" and "IMPROVE" at low opacity over dappled foliage; only "RUN." carries | 🟢 We are legible. ⚠️ **And the gap analysis over-read this**: it proposed replacing ours with "a looping desaturated video". The screenshot shows a **still**; nothing establishes it is video |
| **A week streak on the profile** | `7185`: *0 WEEK STREAK* | 🟢 **Exactly the illusion-of-progress class Wood's binding rulings forbid.** A streak punishes the rest day this product exists to defend |
| **A merchandising profile** | `7185`: a Free Plan card with a three-feature list and "Upgrade to Miles Pro" | 🟢 **We killed this** (SLT, unanimous). Seeing it shipped does not change the ruling: Me is our lowest-frequency surface |
| **The paywall's own brand break** | `7184` | 🟢 §1.2 |
| **Two prices, £24 apart, one tap apart** | `7183` £79.99 → `7184` £55.99 | 🟢 The first price was not the best price, and the user now knows it |

## 4. What we already do BETTER — hold, do not trade away

- **The wizard asks more, and asks it better.** Their day picker is seven circles; **our
  `WeekGrid` derives availability, blackout days AND the long-run day from one control.**
- **Chip vs CardSelect has a written deciding rule** (three questions, `ui-patterns.md`).
  Theirs is consistent by habit; ours is consistent by doctrine.
- **`DurationPicker` / `WheelPicker` are one owner** (FORMS-PRIM-01). They wheel-pick too,
  but we can prove there is no second implementation.
- **Refusals.** Nothing in 17 screenshots shows them declining to generate a plan. We refuse,
  with reasons.
- **Zone discipline as a first-class metric.** Their intensity control is a three-way
  preference; ours is measured against what the runner actually did.

## 5. 🔴 The opportunity the checklist could not see

**Our charity codes have never been redeemed. Not once.** (`GTM-CHARITY-04`.)

Our redeem screen lives on **Me** — the lowest-frequency surface in the product, which the
SLT itself described as the worst place for a conversion moment. **Miles puts the referral
code IN the onboarding flow** (`7179`), between the wizard and the plan, where a code in a
runner's hand is still in their hand.

**That is a placement hypothesis with a measured failure behind it**, and it costs one
screen position, not a build. It is the highest expected-value item in this document and it
was not in any T-slot because the brief filed `7179` as "referral code screen" and we scored
ourselves PARTIAL on having one.

## 6. Board rulings

conversational wizard · weight/height capture · the three-tier plan picker · a trajectory
curve · a cumulative total on PlanArc · celebrating the peak week · an empty-state
illustration · a stock-footage launch screen.

| # | Ruling | |

| **M-1** | **The consequence subtitle becomes a pattern.** Any row that changes a training parameter states **what it does to the runner's training**, not what the field is called | 🟢 SHIP. Strings are the founder's |
| **M-2** | **S6 AMENDED** — icons permitted on a row carrying a **current value**, and on an option whose glyph **encodes the data** (the ability-tier bars). ⛔ Silvanto binding: one glyph per row, one size, one family, one tinted container | 🟢 SHIP WITH AMENDMENT |
| **M-3** | **The dashed-border escape card** is adopted as the grammar for *"a different kind of option that branches"* | 🟢 SHIP |

| **M-5** | **Redeem moves into the onboarding flow.** Measured failure, cheap fix | ⚖️ **SLT** — it is funnel placement, not design |
| **M-6** | **A week streak, a run count as a headline, or any cumulative total** | 🔴 **KILLED, permanent.** Wood's existing ruling extended to the profile surface. Seeing a competitor ship it is not evidence |
| **M-7** | **A merchandising profile** | 🔴 **KILL STANDS** (SLT). Re-examined against `7185` and unchanged |
| **M-8** | **The ticket-stub perforation** | ⚠️ **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE.** Collins for, Silvanto against as chrome. Settles with one artefact: the plan-scale card built both ways, at 375px |

### ⚠️ What this review does not settle

Nothing here has run on a device. **No comparison was made of motion or transitions** — 17
stills cannot show them, and that is likely where a large part of "feeling something" lives.
And the five unreviewed Zonna screens (upgrade, post-run, benchmark, recalibration, reshape)
are still unreviewed; several rulings above are *pending* those sittings.
