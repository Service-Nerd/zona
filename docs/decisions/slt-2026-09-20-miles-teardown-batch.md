## Decision 1 · P-01 semantic colour — **BUILD. Resolution A. Before October.**

Unanimous.

**Sutherland reframed the case and the reframing is the useful output.** This is not
differentiation, it is **costly signalling**: a colour that tells you off loses the
beginner market and cannot be A/B-tested into existence, and that cost is what makes it
credible. **Miles cannot copy it** — not for want of a token, but because it contradicts
what they sell. *"You are buying a moat with a comment in a CSS file."*

**Wood: the strongest item in the batch, and not close.** Colour is a **context cue** —
no reading, no willpower, no interpretation. Learned once, then perceived pre-consciously
forever. *That is how habits change.* ⚠️ **But retrospective colour changes nothing.**
The version that changes behaviour is the ceiling **at the moment of decision**, which is
**P-03, not P-01**. P-01 is the vocabulary; P-03 is the intervention.

**Traynor:** reverse cost rises with users and we have almost none. **The cohort is the
reason to do it now, not later.**

**Three binding conditions:**
1. **The sweep is the item**, not the tokens. One predicate, not four copies. *(Fried)*
2. **The "held the zone" threshold goes to the Coaching Board** before the design system
   encodes it. If it is currently whatever number happens to be in the code, that is the
   `MARATHON-VOLUME-GATE-01` / `peakKmByLevel` defect class. *(Hutchinson)*
3. **The free-tier state is a named deliverable**, not a leftover. *(Sutherland/Traynor —
   see the conflict below.)*

## Decision 2 · P-07 coach register — **DON'T BUILD.**

**Wood used the kill mandate.** A tone setting changes no context, adds no friction,
removes no cognitive load and alters no decision. It changes how the app **sounds** while
you do exactly what you were going to do. **Illusion-of-progress class.**

**Fried dismantled the R19 argument, and nobody defended it.** *"R19 was filed as don't
pick up without a product trigger. Finding a trigger is not the same as the trigger being
worth pulling."* ⚠️ **`R19` therefore stays parked** — it is **not** unblocked, because the
thing that would have unblocked it is not being built. The Phase 2 proposal said otherwise
and is wrong.

**Traynor ruled out the paid DHTB register explicitly**, on a reason distinct from the
others: it makes the personal brand a **purchasable component**, which is a dependency on
a person written into the revenue line. `brand.md` says the app must outlive the personal
brand; selling it contradicts that where it counts. **Recorded now so it is not
re-proposed in six months as easy revenue.**

## Decision 3 · P-13 — **SPLIT, as proposed.**

**(c) illustration: commission ONE piece for the ceremony, after P-01. Judge it, then

**Do not design a new elevation system.**

## Decision 4 · Copy patterns — **APPROVE at pattern level.**

String-by-string is how copy drifts from the rule that governs it.

**The zero case — the SLT gives the shape, the founder writes the words.**
*When nothing held the zone, the sentence must carry a cause or a next action, never a
bare count.* **"None held the zone this week" alone is a scold**, and Wood is clear that
shame suppresses the behaviour we want.

Both rejections upheld (*"reading your last 12 weeks"* is false; *"No make-up runs."* is
unverified). The trial-timeline block is upheld pending `TIER-TRIAL-CONFIDENCE-01`.

## Escalation A · the base-build on-ramp — **BUILD IT, BUT NOT FOR OCTOBER.**

**Traynor's asymmetry is decisive and it does not go where the submission expected.** The
downside of admitting them is not churn — it is **shipping unmeasured prescription to ~500
first-time marathoners sourced from a charity partner**, where one injury story
attributed to the app ends a £27,965 relationship and the first referral channel. Against
that, the upside is a few weeks of earlier plans. **Not close.**

**But refusing and offering nothing is also unacceptable.** Wood: a first-time marathoner
told no with nothing attached either gives up or trains anyway with no plan, and **the
second is worse than admitting them** and more likely for someone who has told their
friends. Traynor: a refused runner is **a redeemed code that produced no product**, and
`GTM-CHARITY-06` means we could not even count them.

**Two tracks, deliberately separated:**

1. **For October — small and certain: the refusal gains an ACTION.** Today we refuse and
   offer nothing. No engine change, no board, no measurement gate. Copy plus the existing
   §44 alternatives mechanism. **This is the October deliverable.**
2. **The on-ramp — build behind a flag, generate the cohort, run `measure:fitness` and the
   property sweep, return to the board.** ⚠️ **Set a date**, or it becomes another item
   §111 names and nobody builds.

**Sutherland's note, recorded because it may be the best framing available:** *"We won't
sell you a marathon plan you can't safely do"* is the most on-brand sentence this company
could say to a charity cohort, and the opposite of what every competitor does. **The
problem was never the refusal. It is that we refuse and offer nothing.**

## Escalation B · the Sims pattern — **SPLIT IT. One third is real.**

The Coaching Board asked for this as one pattern. **Hutchinson, wearing the SLT hat, broke
it — and the SLT sides with him.**

> *"Three Sims asks have died. They did not die of the same cause, and bundling them is an
> analytical error."*

| Ask | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle periodisation (ENGINE-03/CA-05) | **stays blocked** | **Contested science** *and* missing data. Hutchinson's scepticism recorded alongside Sims's position, unsynthesised, per ADR-017. |
| `INPUT-SEX-01` | **stays parked** | No formula we hold reads it. **An honest null is not a gap.** |
| **RED-S / energy availability** | 🔴 **REAL, UNCOVERED HARM** | **Not contested at all.** Low energy availability in young female endurance athletes is as settled as this field gets, and we cannot see it. |

**Treating them as one pattern let the real one hide inside the two that are fine.**

**Action:** file RED-S as a **safety item, not a feature** — it does not belong in a
backlog next to comparison pages (Wood). Route the data question properly: it needs
**volume and frequency**, not modality, and **may be answerable from data we already
hold.**

## Conflicts

**Sutherland and Traynor on P-01's free/paid line — resolves into a requirement.** Both
independently reach *"the thesis is invisible to free users"*. Sutherland: it is upside
down — the runner who most needs telling they cooked it is the one still deciding whether
the app is worth anything. Traynor: acceptable **only if the absence is made visible** —
a free user who never meets the concept is a lost conversion; one who sees *"zone check —
Pro"* has been told what they are missing. **Resolved: P-01 approved, free-tier state is a
named deliverable.**

**Wood vs Sutherland on P-07 — does not synthesise.** Sutherland wants Blunt offered at an
earned moment, because the offer itself signals. Wood says that makes confrontation
opt-in and therefore dismissible, which is **worse**. Both right about different users;
neither moves without behavioural data we do not have. **Recorded, not resolved.**

**Hutchinson vs the Coaching Board's own framing on Escalation B.** Resolved in
Hutchinson's favour, above.

**Fried vs the R19 argument.** Undefended. R19 stays parked.
