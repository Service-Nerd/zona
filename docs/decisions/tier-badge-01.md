# TIER-BADGE-01 — the tier badge, and the five states behind three words

**Design Board, 2026-09-29. SHIP WITH AMENDMENT (a,b,c) / DON'T SHIP (d) / AMEND (e).**

## The decision in one line

A runner's access is **five** states, not three, and the screen now says which one — as a
status at the documented micro-label, in two colours, with no state styled above another.

## Why it is not decoration

The founder asked for colour and a "wow moment". The measurement found a **defect**
underneath the request: a charity-comped runner read the same word as a paying
subscriber, and 500 of them receive codes this week. **Colour was the ask; honesty was the
finding.**

## What was deliberately refused

- **The identity-region placement** for the ledger (d). Not physically available.
- **Five colours.** "Five colours on one label is a legend."
- **Any rank.** `Free` is the same label as `Pro` in a different colour.
- **A milestone or level.** `LEVELS-01` was not built; Kaevor #2 stands.
