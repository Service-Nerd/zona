## The board

🧠 **Sutherland.** Selling 100K is a costly signal in itself: it tells a marathoner the app is serious. That value is
being collected whether or not anyone runs one. It does not oblige us to build for runners who are not there.

📦 **Fried.** Fifty-six people, every one with full access, and nobody picked an ultra. The only real ultra plan in the
building belongs to the person who built the app, and his race was in July. Building ratified-but-unbuilt engine work
for no runner is exactly what the gate exists to stop. Gate not met. I would add, since we are being honest: we put
"50K and 100K" on the pricing page while knowing its midweek slot is mis-priced. That is fine at zero users. It stops
being fine at one.

🏃 **Hutchinson.** Coaching hat: correctness is not in doubt and was never the question; the 2026-09-10 ruling stands.
My concern is the shape of the gate. "Acquisition" as worded means the first ultra runner **receives** the Friday tempo
before the board sits, which is the trap ULTRA-VOICE-REPURPOSED-01 already named. The voice fix makes it honest, not
right. I accept deferral, on condition the trigger is the **first plan**, not some cohort size, and that the Coaching
Board sits on that trigger with Seiler's measurement as its first step.

🔬 **Wood.** No behaviour exists to change. Nobody is doing this. A build here would be work for an imagined user.

🧭 **Zhuo.** The gate needs a number or it is a feeling. "Acquisition" has been the gate for ten weeks and nobody could
say what would meet it. Make it observable: an ultra plan generated by an account that is not the demo and not the
founder. That is checkable by the script this sitting already wrote.

## Conflicts

- **Hutchinson vs Fried on what "acquisition" should mean.** Fried's instinct is a cohort; Hutchinson wants the first
  plan, because the first runner is the one harmed. The board takes **the first plan**: one runner is enough to make the
  defect real, and waiting for several means several receive it.
- **Fried's standing objection on selling the distance** is recorded, not acted on: withdrawing 50K/100K from sale is a
  tier/offer change nobody proposed, and at zero users it protects nobody.

## Ruling

**GATE NOT MET. The build stays gated; the gate now has a number.**

- **Re-open trigger (replaces "ultra acquisition"):** the **first 50K or 100K plan generated in production by an account
  that is neither the demo account nor the founder's.** Checked by `scripts/board-evidence-sig-ultra-unbuilt-01.ts`
  (live `plans`, `plan_archive`, and the distance-carrying generation events). No alert is built by this ruling.
- **On that trigger:** the 🏃 **Coaching Board sits** on the slot, **Seiler's measurement first** (delivered
  quality-session share against `INTENSITY_DISTRIBUTION['50K']`), then McMillan's empty-slot option against wiring
  §24e with Willy's three guards. The 2026-09-10 correctness ruling and the `SIG_UNBUILT` register are unchanged.
- **Until then:** no engine work, no Coaching Board sitting on this item.

**Hand-off:** 🏃 Coaching Board, **on the trigger**, not now.

## MUST/NEVER check
Free users unaffected (ultra is PAID). No prescription changes. `SIG_UNBUILT` entries may not be deleted (2026-09-10).

## Risks to existing features
None from this ruling. The standing risk is the one recorded: the first ultra runner receives a labelled Friday tempo in
place of the ultra-specific session until the board sits.

## What this does not prove
Why nobody picks ultra (not measured: no wizard-step funnel by distance). Whether anyone abandoned the wizard at the
distance step because of ultra (not measured). That the founder's account is the July 100K account is inferred from the
race date matching his published race, not from identity data, which this note deliberately does not read.
