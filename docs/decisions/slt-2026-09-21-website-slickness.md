⚠️ **The palette claim, carried forward correctly.** The 2026-09-20 withdrawal of *"their palette is
ours"* **stands**: that was their APP, sampled from screenshot pixels, Δ41.8 on the accent. The
website is a different surface read by a different method. **Always say which surface and which
method.** Conclusion unchanged and better evidenced: warm-neutral plus one green is the category
default and cannot be our differentiator.

**Their commercials, from their own JSON-LD:** $14.99/mo or $99.99/yr, **7-day trial on annual only,
no ongoing free tier.** We are roughly half the price **with** a genuine free tier and nine complete
published plans.

**Their positioning is not ours.** *"Around half of Miles runners are new to running."* They buy
beginner search volume; we sell a behaviour. Overlap is real and growing (Make-A-Wish first-timers,
§118) but narrower than the visual similarity suggests.

### The two rulings that were asked for explicitly

**W-09's colour: near-black `#1A1A1A`, no ADR.** Traynor, unopposed: a deep moss is a new token
value, an ADR-007 amendment, **and it dilutes the meaning P-01 has just assigned to moss**
("held the zone"). Spending moss as a decorative band in the same month it gained a semantic is how
a semantic palette rots. ⚠️ **Also correcting the brief:** Miles's closing band is `#1F3D2E`, a deep
forest green, **not** their accent, and the order is green-then-black, not black-then-green. A band
in our actual `--moss` `#6B8E6B` would be far weaker than the thing that was admired.

**W-03's content: Coaching Board, Hutchinson chairing.** Vehicle approved, outcome claim not his to
grant at the SLT. *"The evidence supports this approach"* and *"you will not get slower"* are
different sentences and the gap between them is where credibility dies. Expect the defensible
version to **name the mechanism rather than promise the result**.

## Conflicts, recorded rather than synthesised

**1. Sutherland versus the premise — the sharpest disagreement.** Their site is polished *because
they sell to anxious beginners*, and reassurance is correctly matched to that buyer. Ours sells to
the overconfident, for whom *"a slightly austere, slightly unimpressed room"* is congruent with
"you're trying hard, that's the problem." He warns the batch risks sanding off the difference.

Wood and Traynor counter that W-06/07/09 are not convergence, they are **accidents**: no hierarchy,
no rhythm, no close. **The board sides with Wood/Traynor on those and with Sutherland on everything
decorative.** The test, applied per item: **is this a deliberate absence or an accident?** Fix
accidents, keep deliberate absences. Film grain is neither, which is why it dies.

**2. Wood versus the brief on W-03, joined independently by Hutchinson.** The brief proposed
answering *"will I get slower?"* with a **promise**. Wood: *"a promise is the weakest available
instrument against an evidence question. The person asking is not short of reassurance, they are
short of proof."* **W-03 and W-04 are the same item and W-04 is the answer.** Not a split — two
lenses converging.

**3. Fried versus Traynor on W-01's timing.** Fried: a hub with two guides is worse than no hub, and
founder writing is the scarcest resource during a live partnership. Traynor: the shelf is nearly
free and its absence delays the third guide. **Resolved: build the shelf, publish at three**, the
same gate the comparisons hub already uses.

**4. Fried versus the process.** *"Eleven items is not a plan, it is a list."* W-06/07/08/10 are one
afternoon and became a programme with a board sitting. **Accepted** — ranked as one item.

**5. Traynor's standing objection, unresolved and recorded.** *"What is the traffic?"* No revenue,
no redeemed-code conversion data, no meaningful marketing analytics. **Every ranking here is a
judgement about plausibility, not evidence, and the record says so rather than pretending
otherwise.** Ranked on asymmetry and durability instead.

## Illusion-of-progress, named as asked

- 🔴 **W-11 only.** Changes no decision, contradicts a documented rule, and the sole argument for it
  is that a competitor has it.
- 🟡 **W-08 and W-10 are real but low-leverage.** Do them inside the craft pass; do not dignify them
  with individual attention.
- 🟢 **W-01 is emphatically NOT busywork**, despite looking the most like it. Wood: someone
  searching *"should my easy runs feel this slow"* at 10pm is **at the exact moment of doubt the
  product exists to resolve. That is context, not motivation** — the most Wood-shaped item on the list.
- 🟢 **W-06 is not taste.** A hero claim at the same size as its section headings is a **cognitive
  load** problem, not an aesthetic one: the reader must work out what is being claimed, and readers
  do not do that work.

## MUST/NEVER

- **W-11 violates "no chrome"** — one of the grounds for the kill.
- **W-08 introduces no colour**: `--card` `#FFFFFF` is an existing token used as a section ground.
- **W-09 as ruled introduces no colour**: `#1A1A1A` is `--ink`, already the CTA band today.
- **W-06 touches ADR-007's type layer** → `frontend-design` + a scale recorded in `ui-patterns.md`.
  ⚠️ **Eyebrow tracking stays at 0.08em** (standardised 2026-09-20, codebase 64:17). Theirs at
  0.14em is explicitly not a reason to reopen it.
- No em dashes; en dashes in ranges are correct; every new marketing surface joins `noEmDash.test.ts`.
- `BRAND.name` and `PRICING` interpolated, never literals. **W-05 is about pricing and is the item
  most likely to hardcode a number.**
- No competitor price on our pages without joining `COMPETITOR_FACTS`.

## Risks to existing features

- **W-06/07/08 touch the shared layer `GTM-SITE-01` unified** (`SiteHeader`/`SiteFooter`,
  `app/page.tsx`, `PlanPage.tsx`, `ComparisonPage.tsx`). **A rhythm change made per-page
  re-fragments exactly what that item fixed. Change the shared layer or nothing.**
- **W-09/W-10 touch `SiteFooter`, which renders on four marketing surfaces.**
- **W-01** adds a third catalogue beside `MARKETING_PLANS` and `COMPARISON_ARTICLES` — follow that
  shape exactly, including a test over the whole catalogue so unwritten pages are pre-covered.
- **W-05 must not restate pricing.** `pricing.test.ts` already fails the build when a
  `PAID_ONLY_ONGOING` gate has no `/pricing` row; a second place claiming what free includes is a
  new drift surface. **Read the gates, do not retype them.**
- ⚠️ **No engine code is touched, which also means `verify`, parity, `cohort:shape` and
  `measure:fitness` CANNOT CATCH A MISTAKE HERE.** The guards that apply are `noEmDash`, `pricing`,
  `uiPatternsIntegrity`, `externalLink` and `comparisons`.
