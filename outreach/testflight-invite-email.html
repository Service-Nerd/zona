Why this exists

Most training apps assume your problem is laziness. Yours probably isn't. If you've got a job and a training plan, the problem is usually the opposite — you go medium-hard on everything. Never properly easy, never properly hard. Every run lands in the same grey middle, and you wonder why you're tired and not getting faster.

That's the whole idea: you're trying hard — that's the problem. Run easy when it's easy. Run hard when it's hard. Hold the zone.

Getting in · about two minutes

1 Install TestFlight from the App Store — it's Apple's free beta app.
2 Open this invite link, tap Accept, then Install.
3 Open Zonna, sign in with Apple or Google, and generate your plan.

What to do first

• Build a plan around your race or distance.
• Connect Strava or Apple Health — the coaching needs your runs to do anything useful.
• Check today's session. That's the daily loop — open it, run it, log it.

A few things worth knowing

• Kit is the coach. He reads your runs and gives honest feedback — no "crushing it," no confetti. If you went too hard, he'll say so.
• Everyone starts with 14 days of full access. After that it eases to a free tier — you won't lose your plan.
• I push updates often. Things will move, change, and occasionally break. If it looks different next week, that's me — not you.
