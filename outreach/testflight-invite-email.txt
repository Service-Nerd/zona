Thanks for testing this with me. Quick context, then how to get in.

WHY THIS EXISTS
Most training apps assume your problem is laziness. Yours probably isn't.
If you've got a job and a training plan, the problem is usually the opposite —
you go medium-hard on everything. Never properly easy, never properly hard.
Every run lands in the same grey middle, and you wonder why you're tired AND
not getting faster.

That's the whole idea: you're trying hard — that's the problem. Run easy when
it's easy. Run hard when it's hard. Hold the zone.

GETTING IN (about two minutes)
1. Install TestFlight from the App Store — it's Apple's free beta app.
2. Open the invite link I've sent you, tap Accept, then Install.
3. Open Zonna, sign in with Apple or Google, and generate your plan.

WHAT TO DO FIRST
- Build a plan around your race or distance.
- Connect Strava or Apple Health — the coaching needs your runs to do anything useful.
- Check today's session. That's the daily loop: open it, run it, log it.

A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING
- Kit is the coach. He reads your runs and gives honest feedback — no "crushing
  it," no confetti. If you went too hard, he'll say so.
- Everyone starts with 14 days of full access. After that it eases to a free
  tier — you won't lose your plan.
- I push updates often. Things will move, change, and occasionally break. If it
  looks different next week, that's me — not you.

THE ONE THING I NEED FROM YOU
Tell me what's confusing, broken, or annoying. Don't be polite about it — rough
feedback is the entire point of a beta. Just reply to this email.
