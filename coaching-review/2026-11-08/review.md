   binding" means when the caps differ per day.** `INV-PLAN-VOLUME-SHORTFALL-DECLARED` shares the
   producer's predicate, so both move in one commit either way.

2. **`HR-SAVE-UNEDITED-CONFIRMS-01`** — a save stamps `max_hr_source: 'user_confirmed'`, and §50's
   own copy promises exactly that (*"If {typed} really is your max, tap Save to confirm it"*, landed
   in the same commit as the stamp). The open case is a runner who saves having edited only their
   RESTING HR: that promotes a device-observed FLOOR to a confirmed max, which §50's asymmetry
   exists to prevent. The warning is on screen whenever the max is floored, so the runner has been
   told; **whether being told is sufficient is the board's call.** Live: 53 `user_settings` rows,
   37 with a max, **24 `observed`**, **3 below their Tanaka estimate**, 0 above the 15% tolerance.
   ⚠️ If the board wants an explicit act, the remedy is a touched-field flag, **not `editedMax`** —
   re-typing the same number reads as untouched.
