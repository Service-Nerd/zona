Brand visibility audit

The app is named Zonna.
The zones are barely visible.

Zonna's entire reason to exist is zone discipline. The brand promise — "Hold the zone" — is the voice anchor, the differentiator, the thing that makes the app worth paying for. But in the product itself, zones are everywhere a chip, nowhere a headline. A first-time user could be using any running app and not notice this one is different. We need zones to shout, not whisper.

What we do right

Keep

Me screen Zone education

5 zones with personal HR ranges, tap-to-learn drill-downs, brand-voice copy. Genuinely excellent. The asset exists — it's just in the wrong place.

Keep

Coach screen Zone Discipline metric

One of four stats on the Coach grid. Quantifies the promise. "You held the zone 84% of the time" is concrete proof of the app's job.

Keep

Generating ceremony zone line

"Calculating your Zone 2 ceiling. Lower than you'd expect." — a brand moment during the wait. Honest, dry, useful. Don't touch.

Keep

Session colour system

Every session type has a token in lib/session-types.ts that maps to a zone colour. Visual consistency on accent bars, chips, and the upcoming Zone Bar all flow from the same source.

Gaps

Where the brand falls short

Impact: high

No zone intro at the post-plan moment

OrientationScreen mentions only Zone 2 ceiling. The user just generated their plan — this is the ONE moment to explain the framework that plan operates in. Five zones. Personal HR ranges. "Hold the line."

Impact: high

The zone is invisible beneath the Today hero

"10km, slowly." implies Zone 2 but never names it. The hero is the right place for "slowly" (sensory, accessible), but there's no secondary surface naming the zone explicitly. The session card's 11px zone chip is too quiet to anchor the daily prescription.

Impact: high

The voice anchor is invisible in-product

"Hold the zone." appears only on the marketing site and inside AI-generated coach copy. No UI element uses it as an eyebrow, header, or any other visible label. The brand's signature phrase has no in-app home.

Impact: medium

Zones buried in Profile

HRZonesSection is excellent content, but it sits below race benchmark in MeScreen. First-time users may never find it. A user shouldn't need to dig through settings to understand the central concept.

Impact: medium

Zone chip too quiet on session surfaces

11px coloured pill. Same visual weight as the duration label next to it. On the screen that exists to sell the prescription, the zone should be the loudest thing, not just one of several details.

Impact: medium

Wizard collects HR without explaining why

The HR input field note says "Used to calculate your training zones. Kept private." — but nowhere does the wizard tell the user that zone discipline is the product's whole point. Missed teaching moment.

Ship order

Recommendations

Four interventions, ordered by brand impact. Each shippable independently.

  1. Pass 1 — Post-plan-gen zone intro screen. M · ~1 day New ZoneIntroScreen shown once, after first plan generation, between OrientationScreen and the dashboard. Shows the full 5-zone system with personal HR ranges and a "Your home" tag on Zone 2. First visible use of "Hold the zone" in the product. Replaces the current single Zone 2 line on OrientationScreen. Highest brand impact — teaches the system at the moment the user is most receptive.
  2. Pass 2 — "Hold the zone" eyebrow + zone-aware session card on Today. S · ~½ day Hero stays as-is — "10km, slowly." keeps the moss adverb slot. "Slowly" can be felt; "Zone 2" has to be learned. The hero is the experience; the prescription gets its own real estate. Add a small "Hold the zone · Zone 2 today" eyebrow under the hero (first daily use of the voice anchor). Promote the session card's zone chip to a full row: "Zone 2 · aerobic · 113–145 bpm". The prescription is named explicitly without sacrificing the hero's accessibility.
  3. Pass 3 — Zone bar component + bigger zone presence on session card and detail. M · ~2 days New ZoneBar component (5 segments, active one filled in zone colour). Add to session card on Today and Plan. Promote the zone chip on Session Detail to a full prescription card with 28px zone label + HR range + zone bar. New canonical Pattern 21 added to ui-patterns.md.
  4. Pass 4 — Bring Zones up from Profile. S · ~½ day Surface the "Zones" section as its own MeScreen card pinned near the top, not buried below race benchmark. Or add a "Your zones" quick-action from Today (small text link below the session card: "See all your zones →"). The asset is great; the discoverability isn't.
