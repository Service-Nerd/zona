Zonna — Wizard Redesign · frontend-design pass · 2026-08-30

One question per screen. A thin moss line for progress. Two teaching moments at the seams.

Every screen shares one anatomy — back arrow, progress line, question, a dry one-line "why", one tactile control, and a sticky Continue. The new CI-2 primitives (wheel, ruler, day-grid) carry the screens that matter. This is the Zonna reading of Planzy's shape: calmer, no gamification, no counting. Warm Slate throughout, Option A (day selection, no engine change). Copy is draft-final from the spec.

The sequence · screens 0–14

FREE = screens 0–11. PAID appends 12–14. The two teaching interstitials (ⓘA, ⓘB) sit at the seams — ⓘA reframes expectations right after the goal is stated; ⓘB lands the easy-day lesson once we know the runner's pace, right before they commit their week.

ⓘ B The easy-day lesson Keystone
The easy day

Easy should feel easy.

Most runners push their easy days and coast their hard ones — so every run lands in the same tiring middle. Even elites spend about 80% of their time truly easy. Your easy runs build the engine. Let them.

Notes for review. Progress is a moss line only — never a "step N of M". No auto-advance: selecting highlights, Continue commits (the principled divergence from Planzy). The wheel fades non-centre rows by opacity (no colour gradients — banned). The ruler reads as a real ruler via varying tick heights + a big metric-pair readout above the thumb. The day-grid folds today's two scheduling steps into one tactile control (Rest / Run / Long, with the Long marker constrained to Sat/Sun for first ship). All strings are the spec's draft-final Warm Slate voice; every colour comes from the :root tokens.
