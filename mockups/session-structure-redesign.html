What we took from Runna — and what we didn't

The reference is Runna's "800m into 400m" session view. Here's the calibrated read.

✓ Stolen

  • Per-section cards — each phase chunked, far more scannable than one card of dividers.
  • Numbered steps + connector line — "step 3 of 6" mental model for following along mid-run.
  • Plain-language cues — "Conversational. No faster than 5:53/km", "Easy — let the HR drop." Pure Zonna voice.
  • ⓘ on jargon — tap the main-set info to open the existing zone-education sheet.
  • Standalone rest / lead-in as its own numbered step (the hill approach run).

✕ Skipped (on purpose)

  • Solid colour section bars — "flood, not accent" is banned by ADR-007. We tint the header instead.
  • Pace-only single targets — Runna has no zone. Zones are our brand; we keep zone + pace band.
  • Figurative run/walk icons — the dot + role + colour already carries work-vs-rest; icons fight our restraint.
  • RUN / REST action tags — our richer role words (Hard / Jog / Stand / Uphill) already say it.

Broken main-set text (hill reps)

Just fix

describeDerivedSet() in lib/plan/resolveMainSet.ts is a naïve length + pace + modality concatenation → "until ready stand", "same as the 1:30 … jog", downhill dropped. The step-row layout replaces the string entirely — restores ADR-019's intent. No board.

Metric follows the toggle · distance restored · plain-language cues

Just fixDesign

Distance leads (the toggle is on km); time stays as a cue on rep rows since interval work is clock-native. Reuses the per-session metric plumbing (commit 23e2331). The conversational qualifiers are copy — Zonna voice, no board.

Per-section cards + numbered steps

Design

New pattern for ui-patterns.md — "Session steps": per-phase card, tinted header (session colour, low opacity — never flooded), numbered steps with a connector, right-stacked metric, ⓘ → zone education. Warm-up / cool-down pace reuses the runner's easy band.

Header zone vs Kit tile contradiction

Coaching boardthen fix

Two writers. The header computes zone from session.type, and zoneNumberForType() collapses every quality session to Zone 3; the Kit tile is frozen AI prose that wrote "Zone 4–5, 158–185 bpm". On intervals / hills Kit is arguably more honest. One board question: what zone does the header show for a mixed-intensity quality session? Then one source feeds both surfaces.

Warm Slate + Inter, single light theme (ADR-008). Distances are indicative conversions of each traced session's own targets; the app computes them from pace. Verified no row clips at 347px phone width.
